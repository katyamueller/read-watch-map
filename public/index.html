Read & Watch Map

Where your books and films come from.

📚 Goodreads

Syncing only gets the 100 books most recently added to your “read” shelf, and your profile has to be public. For your full history, go to My Books → Import and export → Export Library and drop the CSV file here.

🎬 Letterboxd

Syncing only gets your ~50 most recent diary entries. For your full history, go to Settings → Data → Export your data and drop the ZIP file here.

Everything stays in your browser. To find countries, the app sends titles and author names to Wikidata and Open Library.
